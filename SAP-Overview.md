# SAP Overview

SAP is a leading enterprise software suite used for business process management and enterprise resource planning (ERP). It helps organizations manage finance, supply chain, manufacturing, sales, procurement, and human resources in a unified system.

## Core purpose
- Centralize business data and processes
- Improve operational efficiency
- Support decision-making with real-time reporting
- Standardize business workflows across departments

## Common SAP products
- SAP ERP: Core business processes
- SAP S/4HANA: Modern ERP platform built on in-memory processing
- SAP BW/4HANA: Data warehousing and analytics
- SAP MM: Materials Management
- SAP SD: Sales and Distribution
- SAP FICO: Finance and Controlling
- SAP HR/HCM: Human Capital Management

## Key benefits
- Integrated data across departments
- Better visibility into operations
- Improved compliance and reporting
- Scalable architecture for growing businesses

## Typical implementation focus
- Business process analysis
- Data migration
- Configuration and customization
- Integration with third-party systems
- Security, roles, and authorization
- User training and support
