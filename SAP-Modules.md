# SAP Modules

SAP solutions are organized into modules that support different business functions. Each module can work independently or as part of a connected enterprise platform.

## Main functional modules

### SAP FI (Financial Accounting)
- General ledger
- Accounts payable and receivable
- Asset accounting
- Financial reporting

### SAP CO (Controlling)
- Cost center accounting
- Profitability analysis
- Internal orders
- Budgeting and planning

### SAP MM (Materials Management)
- Procurement
- Inventory management
- Vendor management
- Invoice verification

### SAP SD (Sales and Distribution)
- Sales order processing
- Pricing and billing
- Shipping and delivery
- Customer support and returns

### SAP PP (Production Planning)
- Planned orders
- Work centers
- Bills of material
- Manufacturing execution

### SAP WM / EWM (Warehouse Management)
- Storage bin management
- Goods receipt and issue
- Inventory movement tracking
- Warehouse optimization

### SAP HCM / SuccessFactors
- Recruitment
- Payroll
- Time management
- Employee development

## Cross-module considerations
- Master data consistency
- Integration between modules
- Reporting and analytics
- Authorization and segregation of duties

## Best practices
- Align process design with business goals
- Keep master data clean and standardized
- Document interfaces and dependencies
- Monitor performance and user adoption
